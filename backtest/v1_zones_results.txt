Method
- v1 live signal is 30s–10m mid-price momentum on 1.5s ticks.
  No ticks were on disk, so the mid path is linear close-to-close
  inside each 5m bar at 1.5s steps. This is an approximation.
- Zones use v3 15m order-block rules (not fair-value gaps).
  Touch / mitigation uses 5m wicks. A zone breaks on a 15m close
  through the far side.
- $0.30 spread, 0.01 lot ($1 P/L per $1), one position at a time.
  Exits walk 5m bars; stop is counted first if both are hit.
- SL and TP match v1's paper card: SL = 0.05% of entry,
  TP1 = 1.5 × 5m ATR(14). (The on-page "Stop (1.5×ATR)" figure
  is the TP1 distance, not the stop.)
- In this window, median SL ≈ $2.08 and median TP1 ≈ $6.07
  (median R:R ≈ 2.9). Gold fell from about 4372 to 3515.
